Instant Pot Mashed Potatoes

Potatoes pressure-cooked right in chicken broth, then mashed with butter, cream and sour cream for a rich, smooth side. One Instant Pot, no separate boil-and-drain.

~40 min·serves 4·4 servings (from 800g potatoes)
Pairs well withHoney Lemon Garlic Chicken ThighsLemon Butter Shrimp

Ingredients

Servings
4
  • potatoes — Yukon gold, washed and quartered; peel or leave the skins on to taste
  • garlic cloves — smashed and roughly chopped
  • chicken broth — cooks with the potatoes, then thins the mash to taste
  • salted butter — if using unsalted, add a pinch more salt
  • heavy cream — or half-and-half
  • sour cream — or full-fat yogurt, Greek yogurt, or cream cheese
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • garlic powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • dried chives — or dried dill, for garnish

Steps

~40 min start to finish · 2 phases
CookMash
0–4m

  • Cook

    Add the quartered potatoes, chopped garlic and chicken broth to the Instant Pot inner pot and secure the lid.

    4 min

4–28m

  • Cook

    Set the valve to Sealing and cook on manual High Pressure for 14 minutes (15 minutes at high altitude).

    24 min · unattended

28–30m

  • Cook

    Carefully quick-release the pressure, then remove the lid. Leave on Keep Warm, or turn off, until you are ready to mash.

    2 min · unattended

30–32m

  • Mash

    For smoother potatoes, leave all the broth in the pot. For thicker potatoes, drain off about a third of the broth and reserve it to thin the mash later.

    2 min

32–37m

  • Mash

    Mash the potatoes with a potato masher to the consistency you like, then stir in the butter, heavy cream, sour cream, salt, black pepper, garlic powder and Italian seasoning until combined.

    5 min

37–40m

  • Mash

    Adjust the texture: if too thick, stir in a little more cream or the reserved broth; if too thin, cook on the Saute function, stirring, until it tightens. Top with the dried chives or dill to serve.

    3 min

One Instant Pot: the potatoes cook submerged in the chicken broth, which doubles as the liquid to thin the mash, so there is no separate boil and drain. Draining about a third of the broth before mashing gives a thicker mash; keep it all for a looser, smoother one. Hold finished potatoes on Keep Warm with the lid on, stirring in a little more cream before serving if they tighten up.
