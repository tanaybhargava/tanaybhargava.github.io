Instant Pot Calrose Rice

Plain short-grain Calrose rice in the Instant Pot, no sushi vinegar, just plump, tender grains with good definition. A tight 1:1.1 water ratio accounts for short-grain absorption.

~28 min·serves 4·4 servings (from 300g dry rice)

Ingredients

Servings
4
  • Calrose rice — short-grain, rinsed until the water runs clear, well drained300 g
  • water — 1:1.1 by weight to the dry rice330 g
  • butter — about 1 tsp, neutral oil or butter, optional, stops starchy foaming5 g
  • salt — about 1/2 tsp, optional2 g

Steps

~28 min start to finish · 1 phase
Cook
0–3m

  • Cook

    Rinse the rice in a fine-mesh strainer 3 to 4 times under cold water until it runs clear, to wash off surface starch so the rice does not turn gummy. Drain very well.

    3 min

3–4m

  • Cook

    Add the rinsed rice, water, butter and salt to the Instant Pot inner pot and spread the rice into an even, submerged layer.

    1 min

4–14m

  • Cook

    Secure the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and cook on High Pressure for 5 minutes.

    10 min · unattended

14–24m

  • Cook

    Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes (short-grain needs this carryover steam to finish hydrating), then vent any remaining pressure.

    10 min · unattended

24–28m

  • Cook

    Open the lid, let steam escape for 1 to 2 minutes, fluff gently with a paddle, and leave the lid ajar with Keep Warm off for a few minutes to let surface moisture evaporate.

    4 min · unattended

Ratio: water (g) = dry rice (g) x 1.1. Short-grain absorbs tightly, so this is less water than long-grain rice. The scaler holds the 1:1.1 ratio as you resize. Pressure time stays 5 min + 10 min NPR regardless of batch size; do not skip the natural release or the grains blow out.
