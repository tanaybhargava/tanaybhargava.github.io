Palak Paneer

Soft paneer in a smooth spinach gravy built on a cashew-onion puree. The two purees come together on separate burners, then the curry finishes fast so the spinach keeps its fresh green color.

~32 min·serves 4·4 servings
Pairs well withHand-Pounded Sona Masoori RiceHome-Ground Atta RotiInstant Pot Calrose Rice

Ingredients

Servings
4

Onion puree

  • red onion — chopped
  • ginger — about 1/2 inch, chopped
  • garlic cloves — chopped
  • green chilies — 2 to 4, chopped, to taste
  • cashew nuts
  • salt — for the onion puree
  • water — cooks with the onion then blends in

Spinach puree

  • spinach — palak

Curry

  • oil
  • tomatoes — finely chopped
  • salt — to taste, for the curry
  • black salt — kala namak
  • garam masala
  • kasoori methi — dried fenugreek leaves, crushed between your palms
  • paneer — cut into small cubes and soaked in warm water
  • heavy cream — or fresh cream or malai

Steps

~32 min start to finish · 3 phases
Onion pureeSpinach pureeCurry
0–10m

  • Onion puree

    Put the onion, ginger, garlic, green chilies, cashews, a pinch of salt and the water in a saucepan over medium heat. Cook about 15 minutes until the onion is soft and translucent.

    16 min · unattended

  • Spinach puree

    On another burner, bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil (start it while the onions cook), enough to submerge the spinach. Add the spinach, pushing the leaves under, and blanch exactly 4 minutes.

    10 min · unattended

10–16mmeanwhile: Onion puree still going

  • Spinach puree

    Lift the leaves straight into ice-cold water to stop the cooking, then drain and blend into a smooth puree.

    4 min

16–19m

  • Onion puree

    Cool slightly, then blend with the cooking water into a smooth puree. Set aside (reuse the blender for the spinach).

    3 min

19–24m

  • Curry

    Heat the oil in a pan over medium. Add the tomatoes and cook until soft and mushy, mashing with the back of the spatula.

    5 min

24–27m

  • Curry

    Add the onion puree, mix, and simmer 2 to 3 minutes without browning the gravy.

    3 min

27–28m

  • Curry

    Stir in the remaining salt, black salt, garam masala and crushed kasoori methi.

    1 min

28–30m

  • Curry

    Add the spinach puree, mix, and adjust the consistency with a little water if needed. Simmer only 2 minutes so the gravy keeps its green color.

    2 min

30–32m

  • Curry

    Add the drained paneer and the cream, mix, bring to a gentle simmer, then turn off the heat right away.

    2 min

Start the spinach-blanching water on a second burner while the onion mixture cooks, so the two purees finish together. Keep the final simmer short once the spinach puree goes in, long cooking dulls the bright green. Soak the paneer cubes in warm water so they stay soft.
