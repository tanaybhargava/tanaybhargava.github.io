Lemon Butter Shrimp

Garlic shrimp in a lemon butter pan sauce, served over rice and peas.

Pairs well withInstant Pot Calrose RiceInstant Pot Mashed Potatoes
Servings
4

About 8 shrimp with sauce per person, as a main.

⏱️11 min

Ingredients

  • medium shrimp — peeled and deveined, about 8 per person
  • garlic cloves — minced, about 1 clove per serving
  • chicken stock
  • lemon juice — fresh, plus a little finely grated lemon zest
  • unsalted butter — fridge-cold, cut into small cubes
  • oil — or a little butter, for searing

Steps

~11 min start to finish · 1 phase
Shrimp and sauce
0–4m

  • Shrimp and sauce

    Sear the shrimp with the minced garlic in a small splash of oil or butter until pink and opaque (about 120 to 145F / 49 to 63C). Set the shrimp aside on a plate.

    4 min

4–7m

  • Shrimp and sauce

    Pour the stock, lemon juice and zest into the hot pan. Simmer on medium-high for 2 to 3 minutes until reduced by half to a syrupy few tablespoons.

    3 min

7–8m

  • Shrimp and sauce

    Turn the heat off completely (or pull the pan off the burner).

    1 min

8–10m

  • Shrimp and sauce

    Whisk in the cold butter one cube at a time, letting each melt into a creamy, pale-yellow emulsion before adding the next (monter au beurre).

    2 min

10–11m

  • Shrimp and sauce

    Toss the shrimp back in briefly to coat, then spoon everything over warm rice and peas.

    1 min

  • The sauce is equal parts chicken stock, lemon juice and cold butter.
