Lemon Butter Shrimp

Seared garlic shrimp in a glossy lemon butter pan sauce, built on a simple 4:4:4 ratio of stock, lemon juice, and cold butter emulsified off the heat. Spoon over warm rice and peas.

~11 min·serves 4·4 servings (32 shrimp, about 6 tbsp sauce)

Ingredients

Servings
4
  • medium shrimp — peeled and deveined, about 8 per person32 piece≈ 2¼ shrimps
  • garlic — minced, about 1 clove per serving4 piece≈ ¾ cloves
  • chicken stock4 tbsp
  • lemon juice — fresh, plus the finely grated zest of half a lemon4 tbsp
  • unsalted butter — fridge-cold, cut into small cubes4 tbsp
  • oil — or a little butter, for searing1 tsp

Steps

~11 min start to finish · 1 phase
Shrimp and sauce
0–4m

  • Shrimp and sauce

    Sear the shrimp with the minced garlic in a small splash of oil or butter until just pink and cooked through. Set the shrimp aside on a plate.

    4 min

4–7m

  • Shrimp and sauce

    Pour the stock, lemon juice and zest into the hot pan. Simmer on medium-high for 2 to 3 minutes until reduced by half to a syrupy few tablespoons.

    3 min

7–8m

  • Shrimp and sauce

    Turn the heat off completely (or pull the pan off the burner).

    1 min

8–10m

  • Shrimp and sauce

    Whisk in the cold butter one cube at a time, letting each melt into a creamy, pale-yellow emulsion before adding the next (monter au beurre).

    2 min

10–11m

  • Shrimp and sauce

    Toss the shrimp back in briefly to coat, then spoon everything over warm rice and peas.

    1 min

The sauce is a 4:4:4 ratio: equal tablespoons of chicken stock, lemon juice and cold butter, yielding about 6 tbsp of sauce (about 1.5 tbsp per person). Keep the butter fridge-cold and mount it off the heat so it emulsifies into a creamy sauce instead of splitting. Pairs with the Instant Pot rice recipes and peas.
