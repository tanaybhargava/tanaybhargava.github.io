Mediterranean Turkey Burgers

Greek-style turkey burgers with roasted red pepper and feta, a cucumber yogurt sauce, and a quick cucumber salad, built as a burger with cucumber slices inside.

~27 min·serves 4·4 burgers

Ingredients

Servings
4

Patties

  • ground turkey565 g
  • red onion — finely diced75 g
  • roasted red peppers — packed in water, drained120 g
  • fat free feta — crumbled40 g
  • breadcrumbs30 g
  • dried parsley1 tbsp
  • dried oregano0.3 tsp
  • dried dill0.5 tsp

Base

  • salt — for the patties0.5 tsp
  • cucumber — about a third grated for the sauce, the rest sliced for the salad and burger3
  • olive oil spray — for the pan and the salad1 pinch

Yogurt sauce

  • plain nonfat Greek yogurt120 g
  • lemon — juiced0.5
  • garlic cloves — minced1

Cucumber salad

  • cherry tomatoes — halved280 g
  • red wine vinegar30 g

Steps

~27 min start to finish · 4 phases
PattiesYogurt sauceCucumber saladAssemble
0–4m

  • Patties

    Quarter the red onion, then pulse it with the roasted red peppers in a food processor until finely minced.

    4 min

  • Yogurt sauce

    Peel and grate about a third of the cucumber on a box grater for the sauce; mince the garlic.

    4 min

  • Cucumber salad

    Peel and slice the remaining cucumber; thinly slice the rest of the red onion; halve the cherry tomatoes.

    6 min

4–6mmeanwhile: Cucumber salad still going

  • Patties

    Add the turkey, breadcrumbs, the processed pepper-onion mixture, feta, salt, dill, oregano and dried parsley to a bowl and mix until just blended.

    4 min

  • Yogurt sauce

    Stir the grated cucumber, yogurt, garlic, a squeeze of half a lemon, dill and salt together in a small bowl.

    2 min

6–8mmeanwhile: Patties still going

  • Cucumber salad

    Toss the cucumber, onion and tomatoes with the red wine vinegar, a spritz of olive oil, salt, oregano and dill.

    2 min

8–12m

  • Patties

    Weigh the batch and divide into equal balls, one per serving. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium heat and spray with oil.

    4 min

12–14m

  • Patties

    Drop the balls onto the pan and smash into patties with a spatula (the mix is sticky).

    2 min

14–24m

  • Patties

    Cook, flipping as needed, until cooked through and the internal temperature reaches 165°F / 74°C.

    10 min

24–27m

  • Assemble

    Build each burger: patty, a spoonful of yogurt sauce, and a few cucumber slices inside the bun. Serve the cucumber salad alongside.

    3 min

Make the yogurt sauce and cucumber salad while the patties cook; all three finish together, then assemble. The cucumber does double duty: grated into the sauce, sliced into both the burger and the salad.
