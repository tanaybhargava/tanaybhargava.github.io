Coconut Milk Jelly

A light, wobbly coconut milk jelly set in jars, somewhere between panna cotta and jelly. Mostly hands-off: a few minutes on the stove, then it sets overnight ready for toppings.

~8 hr 15 min·serves 5·About 5 jars (2/3 cup each)

Ingredients

Servings
5
  • coconut water — about a quarter to bloom the gelatin, the rest heats with the sugar
  • granulated sugar
  • gelatin powder
  • coconut milk
  • whole milk
  • heavy cream

Steps

~8 hr 15 min start to finish · 1 phase
Make and set
0–5m

  • Make and set

    In a small bowl, stir the gelatin powder into about a quarter of the coconut water and set aside to bloom.

    5 min · unattended

5–10m

  • Make and set

    In a small saucepan, heat the remaining coconut water with the sugar until the sugar dissolves and the liquid is just about to boil.

    5 min

10–11m

  • Make and set

    Stir the bloomed gelatin into the hot pot until completely dissolved.

    1 min

11–13m

  • Make and set

    Add the coconut milk, whole milk and heavy cream and whisk to combine.

    2 min

13–493m

  • Make and set

    Pour into jars (about 2/3 cup each, leaving room for toppings, one jar per serving), cover, and chill in the fridge overnight to set.

    8 hr · unattended

493–495m

  • Make and set

    The next day, add any toppings you like and serve.

    2 min

About 2/3 cup per jar, one jar per serving, leaving headroom for toppings. Bloom the gelatin in cold coconut water first so it dissolves smoothly into the hot pot. The overnight chill is what sets it, so start it a day ahead.
