Green Pea and Cheese Pinwheels

Puff pastry rolled around a spiced green pea, potato and cheese filling, sliced into pinwheels and baked.

Servings
8
1 hr 47 min

Five or six pinwheels per person, as an appetizer.

Estimated from the ingredients, for one serving.

Ingredients

  • puff pastry - thawed per the package, kept cold
  • frozen green peas
  • potato - small, boiled and peeled
  • shredded Mexican blend cheese - or Pepperjack
  • golden raisins - roughly chopped; or diced soft dates or dried cranberries
  • fennel seeds - whole
  • ginger - finely grated
  • green chilies - finely chopped, to taste
  • lemon juice - fresh
  • coriander powder
  • cumin powder
  • salt - to taste
  • sugar
  • all-purpose flour - for dusting
  • water - for sealing the pastry

Steps

0 min - 5 min

  • Pastry

    Step 1

    Thaw the puff pastry following the package, keeping it in the fridge so it stays cold.

  • Potato

    Step 2

    Boil the potato whole in salted water until a knife slides in easily.

  • Prep

    Step 3

    While the potato boils, microwave the peas for 1 minute, drain them thoroughly and pat them very dry with paper towels. Wet peas make a soggy filling.

5 min - 8 minmeanwhile: Pastry, Potato still going

  • Prep

    Step 4

    Dry-toast the fennel seeds in a small pan over medium heat for 30 to 45 seconds until aromatic, then crush them coarsely in a mortar and pestle or under the flat of a knife.

8 min - 20 minmeanwhile: Pastry, Potato still going

  • Prep

    Step 5

    Grate the ginger, finely chop the green chilies, roughly chop the raisins and juice the lemon.

20 min - 23 minmeanwhile: Pastry still going

  • Potato

    Step 6

    Drain, let it cool enough to handle, and peel.

23 min - 26 minmeanwhile: Pastry still going

  • Filling

    Step 7

    Pulse about three quarters of the peas in a mini blender with the ginger, green chilies and lemon juice into a thick, mostly smooth paste.

26 min - 29 minmeanwhile: Pastry still going

  • Filling

    Step 8

    In a mixing bowl, coarsely smash the rest of the peas with the potato using a fork.

29 min - 33 minmeanwhile: Pastry still going

  • Filling

    Step 9

    Add the pea paste, crushed fennel, coriander, cumin, salt, sugar, raisins and cheese, and stir until well combined. Taste and adjust the salt or lemon.

33 min - 37 min

  • Roll

    Step 10

    On a lightly floured surface, roll one pastry sheet to about 33 x 23 cm / 13 x 9 inches. Spread an even share of the filling over it, leaving a 1 cm / half-inch bare strip along the far long edge.

37 min - 40 min

  • Roll

    Step 11

    Dab water along the bare edge. Starting from the long edge nearest you, roll tightly into a log and press the wet seam to seal.

40 min - 47 min

  • Roll

    Step 12

    Repeat with each remaining sheet and the rest of the filling.

47 min - 1 hr 12 min

  • Chill

    Step 13

    Chill the logs on a tray in the fridge for 25 to 30 minutes, or 15 in the freezer. Cold butter layers slice cleanly without squeezing out the filling.

  • Oven

    Step 14

    While the logs chill, preheat the oven to 200C / 400F and line two large baking sheets with parchment.

1 hr 12 min - 1 hr 17 min

  • Bake

    Step 15

    Slice the logs into 1 cm / half-inch pinwheels with a sharp knife, end pieces included.

1 hr 17 min - 1 hr 21 min

  • Bake

    Step 16

    Lay them flat on the parchment, 4 to 5 cm / 1.5 to 2 inches apart so the pastry can puff.

1 hr 21 min - 1 hr 43 min

  • Bake

    Step 17

    Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, rotating the sheets top to bottom and front to back halfway (do not flip the pinwheels), until deep golden, flaky at the edges and bubbling in the center.

1 hr 43 min - 1 hr 47 min

  • Bake

    Step 18

    Rest on the tray for 3 to 5 minutes.
