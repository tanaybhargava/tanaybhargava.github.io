Chicken and Gravy

Pan-seared chicken cutlets in a white wine cream gravy built from the fond.

Pairs well withInstant Pot Mashed Potatoes
Servings
4

One to two cutlets with gravy per person, as a main.

52 min

Ingredients

Chicken

  • boneless skinless chicken breasts - large, sliced and pounded into cutlets
  • salt - to season the cutlets
  • black pepper - to season the cutlets
  • flour - to dredge the cutlets
  • olive oil - for searing

Deglaze

  • white wine - Sauvignon Blanc

Gravy

  • butter
  • roux flour - plain flour, for the roux

Gravy mixture

  • chicken broth
  • heavy cream - a splash, or half and half
  • Worcestershire sauce - or soy sauce
  • Dijon mustard
  • onion powder
  • garlic powder
  • dried thyme
  • ground sage
  • ground black pepper - for the gravy

Steps

0 min - 4 min

  • Gravy mixture

    Step 1

    In a large measuring cup with a spout, stir together the chicken broth, heavy cream, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard and all the seasonings. Set aside.

4 min - 12 min

  • Chicken

    Step 2

    Slice each breast in half lengthwise into 2 to 3 thin cutlets. Cover with plastic wrap and pound to about 1.5 cm / half an inch thick, then pat dry.

12 min - 16 min

  • Chicken

    Step 3

    Season both sides with salt and pepper, then sprinkle with flour and rub it over the surface so each cutlet is lightly coated.

16 min - 30 min

  • Chicken

    Step 4

    Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear the cutlets in batches, 4 to 5 minutes per side, until golden and 165F / 74C inside. Set aside.

30 min - 34 min

  • Deglaze

    Step 5

    Turn off the heat, pour in the wine, then set the heat to medium. Scrape up the fond with a silicone spatula. Bring to a gentle bubble and reduce by half, about 3 to 4 minutes.

34 min - 37 min

  • Gravy

    Step 6

    Melt the butter into the pan, add the roux flour, and stir continuously for 2 minutes, until the flour turns light brown and the raw smell cooks off.

37 min - 41 min

  • Gravy

    Step 7

    Add the gravy mixture in small splashes, stirring continuously, so the sauce stays thick.

41 min - 47 min

  • Gravy

    Step 8

    Bring to a gentle boil, then drop to a light simmer and let it reduce, uncovered, stirring occasionally, 5 to 7 minutes.

47 min - 52 min

  • Finish

    Step 9

    Return the cutlets and any resting juices to the pan and spoon the sauce over. Simmer very gently for about 5 minutes, until the gravy is as thick as you like. Keep it to a bare simmer so the chicken stays juicy; a hard boil toughens it.

Notes

  • The flour on the cutlets keeps them from sticking and thickens the gravy a little at the end.
  • The longer the gravy simmers, the thicker and more concentrated it gets.
