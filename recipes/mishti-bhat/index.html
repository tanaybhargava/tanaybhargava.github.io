Mishti Bhat (Gobindobhog Sweet Rice)

Bengali sweet rice: fragrant Gobindobhog grains sauteed in ghee, glazed with sugar, pressure cooked at a 1:1.5 rice-to-water ratio, and finished with ghee-fried cashews and plump raisins.

~27 min·serves 4·4 servings (from 300g dry rice)

Ingredients

Servings
4
  • Gobindobhog rice — rinsed and well drained
  • water — 1:1.5 by weight to the dry rice
  • sugar
  • ghee — divided: about a third for the garnish, the rest for sauteing the rice
  • cashews — a handful
  • raisins — a handful

Steps

~27 min start to finish · 3 phases
GarnishRiceFinish
0–1m

  • Garnish

    Heat the garnish portion of the ghee (about a third) in a small pan on the stovetop.

    1 min

  • Rice

    Set the Instant Pot to Saute and add the remaining ghee. Add the rinsed, well-drained rice and saute gently for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant and translucent.

    2 min

1–2mmeanwhile: Rice still going

  • Garnish

    Fry the cashews until lightly golden, then add the raisins and cook until they plump up. Set aside with their ghee to toss in at the end.

    3 min

2–3mmeanwhile: Garnish still going

  • Rice

    Add the sugar directly to the rice and toss for about 30 seconds until it starts to dissolve and glaze the grains.

    1 min

3–12mmeanwhile: Garnish still going

  • Rice

    Turn Saute off. Add the water, scraping the bottom of the pot clean. Seal and cook on High Pressure for 4 minutes.

    9 min · unattended

12–22m

  • Rice

    Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then quick-release the remaining steam.

    10 min · unattended

22–24m

  • Finish

    Open the lid and pour in the fried cashews, raisins and their warm ghee. Fluff gently with a paddle.

    2 min

24–27m

  • Finish

    Leave the lid ajar with Keep Warm off for 3 minutes to vent steam and keep the grains distinct.

    3 min · unattended

Master ratio: water (g) = dry rice (g) x 1.5. Sugar is about 1.5 tbsp (18 to 20g) per 180g (1 cup) of dry rice. The scaler keeps this 1:1.5 ratio so you can size it to 300g, 350g or 400g of rice. Pressure-cook time stays 4 min + 10 min NPR regardless of batch. Fry the cashews and raisins on the stovetop while the Instant Pot does the rice, then combine.
