Gyeran-Ppang (Korean Egg Bread)

Korean street-food egg bread: a lightly sweet vanilla batter baked in a muffin tin with an egg cracked on top of each. Soft, savory-sweet, and eaten hot.

~28 min·serves 4·4 muffins

Ingredients

Servings
4
  • all-purpose flour
  • milk
  • egg — about a third whisked into the batter, the rest beaten for the topping (roughly half an egg per muffin)
  • unsalted butter — melted
  • sugar
  • baking powder
  • vanilla extract
  • salt — kosher
  • cooking oil — for greasing the muffin tin

Steps

~28 min start to finish · 3 phases
Heat ovenMake batterBake
0–10m

  • Heat oven

    Preheat the oven to 400F / 200C and brush one muffin slot per serving with oil. Do this while you whisk the batter.

    10 min · unattended

  • Make batter

    Whisk the melted butter, sugar, salt, baking powder, vanilla, batter egg, flour and milk together until smooth.

    3 min

10–12m

  • Bake

    Divide the batter among the greased slots, filling each about half full.

    2 min

12–13m

  • Bake

    Beat the topping eggs and pour about half an egg onto the center of the batter in each slot.

    1 min

13–27m

  • Bake

    Bake on the middle rack for 13 minutes, or closer to 15 if you want a fully solid egg on top.

    14 min · unattended

27–28m

  • Bake

    Take out of the oven and serve hot.

    1 min

Baked in a muffin tin rather than mini loaf pans: about one muffin per serving, with roughly a third of the egg whisked into the batter and the rest beaten and poured on top (about half an egg per muffin). Bake 13 min for a runnier yolk, 15 for a set egg.
