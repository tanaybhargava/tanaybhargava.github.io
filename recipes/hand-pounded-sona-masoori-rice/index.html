Hand-Pounded Sona Masoori Rice

Everyday Instant Pot rice using hand-pounded Sona Masoori, which keeps its light bran layer and takes a touch more water than polished white rice. Cooks up fluffy with separate grains.

~32 min·serves 4·4 servings (from 300g dry rice)
Pairs well withHoney Lemon Garlic Chicken ThighsPalak Paneer

Ingredients

Servings
4
  • hand-pounded Sona Masoori rice — rinsed until the water runs mostly clear, well drained
  • water — 1:1.8 by weight to the dry rice
  • salt — optional
  • ghee — oil or ghee, keeps grains separate and stops foaming

Steps

~32 min start to finish · 1 phase
Cook
0–3m

  • Cook

    Rinse the rice thoroughly under cold water until it runs mostly clear, then drain well to shed surface moisture.

    3 min

3–4m

  • Cook

    Add the drained rice, water, salt and ghee to the Instant Pot inner pot and spread the rice so it sits submerged and even.

    1 min

4–17m

  • Cook

    Secure the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and cook on High Pressure for 8 minutes.

    13 min · unattended

17–27m

  • Cook

    Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then quick-release any remaining steam.

    10 min · unattended

27–32m

  • Cook

    Open the lid, let steam escape for 1 to 2 minutes, fluff gently with a paddle, and leave the lid ajar with Keep Warm off for 3 to 5 minutes to set the texture.

    5 min · unattended

Ratio: water (g) = dry rice (g) x 1.8 (hand-pounded rice keeps its bran, so it takes a little more water than polished white rice but less than full brown rice). The scaler holds the 1:1.8 ratio as you resize. Pressure time stays 8 min + 10 min NPR regardless of batch size.
