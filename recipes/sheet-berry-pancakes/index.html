Sheet Berry Pancakes

A fluffy buttermilk batter baked flat on a sheet pan, marbled with melted butter and scattered with frozen berries, then flashed under the broiler to caramelize the top. Slice into squares.

~1 hr·serves 4·One 13x11 inch pan, about 4 servings

Ingredients

Servings
4
  • all-purpose flour185 g
  • sugar25 g
  • baking powder6 g
  • baking soda2 g
  • salt2 g
  • whole milk240 g
  • vinegar — stirred into the milk to clabber it into buttermilk15 g
  • egg — 1 large50 g
  • vanilla extract4 g
  • unsalted butter — melted, for the top swirl25 g
  • frozen berries — a generous handful, scattered on top100 g

Steps

~1 hr start to finish · 3 phases
Clabber the milkDry mix and ovenBatter and bake
0–10m

  • Clabber the milk

    Stir the milk and vinegar together in a measuring cup and let it sit on the counter 30 to 45 minutes to thicken and acidify into buttermilk.

    40 min · unattended

  • Dry mix and oven

    Preheat the oven to 400F / 200C and line a 13x11 inch sheet pan with parchment.

    10 min · unattended

10–40mmeanwhile: Clabber the milk still going

  • Dry mix and oven

    Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt together in a large bowl.

    2 min

40–41m

  • Batter and bake

    Whisk the egg and vanilla into the clabbered milk. Keep the melted butter separate for now.

    1 min

41–42m

  • Batter and bake

    Pour the wet mixture into the dry and stir gently with a spatula just until the flour streaks disappear. Keep it lumpy so it stays tender.

    1 min

42–44m

  • Batter and bake

    Spread the batter evenly on the lined pan. Drizzle the melted butter over the top in thin streaks and marble it into the surface with a knife.

    2 min

44–45m

  • Batter and bake

    Scatter the frozen berries across the top.

    1 min

45–57m

  • Batter and bake

    Bake on the middle rack at 400F / 200C for 10 to 12 minutes, until it springs back when gently pressed.

    12 min · unattended

57–59m

  • Batter and bake

    Optional: switch to high broil for the last 45 to 90 seconds to caramelize the butter swirl and bubble the berries. Watch it the whole time so it does not burn.

    2 min

59–60m

  • Batter and bake

    Slice into squares and serve immediately.

    1 min

The vinegar-and-milk sit is what makes the quick buttermilk, so start it first and mix the dry ingredients and heat the oven while it rests. Keep the batter lumpy, overmixing makes it tough. The broiler finish is optional but worth it; it needs constant watching.
