Magnolia Chocolate Chip Cookies

Bakery-style chocolate chip cookies with a chunky, craggy top. A little espresso powder beaten in with the eggs deepens the chocolate.

~1 hr 33 min·serves 40·About 40 cookies

Ingredients

Servings
40
  • all-purpose flour310 g
  • baking soda — heaping6 g
  • sea salt3 g
  • unsalted butter — room temperature113 g
  • light brown sugar — packed360 g
  • eggs — large100 g
  • vanilla extract7 g
  • instant espresso powder — added with the eggs and vanilla2 g
  • semisweet chocolate chips255 g

Steps

~1 hr 33 min start to finish · 2 phases
Make doughBake
0–10m

  • Make dough

    Position a rack in the center of the oven and preheat to 350F / 175C. Line a baking sheet with parchment.

    10 min · unattended

10–12m

  • Make dough

    Whisk the flour, baking soda and salt together in a medium bowl and set aside.

    2 min

12–15m

  • Make dough

    3 min

15–17m

  • Make dough

    Add the eggs, espresso powder and vanilla. Beat on medium until the coffee is fully dissolved and the mixture is smooth and blended.

    2 min

17–18m

  • Make dough

    Add the flour mixture and mix on medium just until incorporated, then a few seconds on high to pull the chunky dough together.

    1 min

18–19m

  • Make dough

    Add the chocolate chips and beat on high about 5 seconds to mix them through.

    1 min

19–30m

  • Bake

    Drop large spoonfuls onto the lined sheet; do not flatten. Bake until lightly browned on top, 10 to 11 minutes.

    11 min · unattended

30–33m

  • Bake

    Cool on the pan for 1 minute, then transfer to a rack. Repeat with the remaining dough.

    3 min

33–93m

  • Bake

    Let the cookies cool completely on the rack, about 1 hour.

    1 hr · unattended

The espresso powder is not for coffee flavor, it deepens the chocolate; 1 tsp is subtle, 2 tsp more noticeable. Dough is meant to be chunky, do not overmix. Store airtight at room temperature up to 3 days.
