Cafe Rio-Style Burrito Bowl

A build-your-own burrito bowl: shredded chile chicken, cilantro-lime dressing, creamy stovetop black beans, and brown rice cooked in the chicken broth. One Instant Pot, so the chicken and rice share the pot in sequence.

~1 hr 10 min·serves 4·4 bowls

Ingredients

Servings
4

Chicken braise

  • chicken breast700 g
  • water250 g
  • soy sauce20 g
  • apple cider vinegar15 g
  • cumin — about 1/4 tsp1.5 g
  • garlic — smashed1 piece≈ ¼ cloves

Rice

  • brown rice — rinsed and drained, cooked in the reserved chicken broth300 g
  • reserved chicken broth — the braising liquid, topped up with water to 1:1.25 by weight to the rice375 g

Chicken glaze

  • red enchilada sauce — mild; about half of a standard 10 oz can150 g≈ ½ cans
  • diced green chiles — one small 4 oz can100 g≈ 1 can
  • red chili powder — about 1 tsp, or cayenne for more heat5 g
  • brown sugar — about 2 tsp10 g
  • butter — about 2 tsp, or neutral oil10 g

Dressing

  • whole milk100 g
  • lime juice — fresh15 g
  • plain Greek yogurt150 g
  • mayonnaise50 g
  • ranch seasoning mix — half a packet14 g
  • tomatillo — 1 small45 g≈ 1 tomatillo
  • fresh cilantro15 g
  • jalapeno — with seeds for heat15 g≈ 1 jalapeno
  • garlic — 1 small clove1 piece≈ ¼ cloves

Beans

  • black beans — one standard 15 oz can, drained1 piece≈ ¼ cans
  • bean spices — cumin, black pepper, oregano, salt, red chili powder, plus a splash of lime to finish1 pinch

Steps

~1 hr 10 min start to finish · 6 phases
Chicken braiseRiceChicken glazeDressingBeansAssemble
0–3m

  • Chicken braise

    Add the chicken breast, water, soy sauce, apple cider vinegar, cumin and smashed garlic to the Instant Pot. Cook on High Pressure for 10 minutes, then 10-minute natural release.

    25 min · unattended

  • Dressing

    Blend the tomatillo, cilantro, jalapeno, dressing garlic, lime juice and about a third of the milk until smooth.

    3 min

  • Beans

    Combine the drained black beans, the reserved splash of chicken broth and the bean spices in a small saucepan and simmer 8 to 10 minutes.

    10 min · unattended

3–10mmeanwhile: Chicken braise, Beans still going

  • Dressing

    Whisk the yogurt, mayo and ranch mix together separately, then stir in the blended aromatics and the remaining milk until uniform. Chill until serving.

    3 min · unattended

10–25mmeanwhile: Chicken braise still going

  • Beans

    Mash about 15% of the beans with a fork while simmering for a creamy gravy, then finish with a splash of lime.

    2 min

25–29m

  • Chicken braise

    Lift out the chicken and shred it. Reserve the braising liquid for the rice (you will cook the rice in the same pot), and set aside a small splash for the beans.

    4 min

29–30m

  • Rice

    In the same Instant Pot, add the rinsed brown rice and the reserved chicken broth, topped up with water to 1:1.25 by weight to the rice. Cook on High Pressure for 22 minutes, then a 10-minute natural release.

    35 min · unattended

  • Chicken glaze

    In a skillet over medium heat, melt the butter and bloom the chili powder for about 30 seconds.

    1 min

30–33mmeanwhile: Rice still going

  • Chicken glaze

    Add the enchilada sauce, green chiles and brown sugar; simmer 2 to 3 minutes.

    3 min

33–64mmeanwhile: Rice still going

  • Chicken glaze

    Toss in the shredded chicken and stir constantly until the sauce reduces and tightly grips the meat.

    4 min

64–67m

  • Rice

    Fluff the rice and leave the lid ajar while you finish the glaze.

    3 min · unattended

67–70m

  • Assemble

    Build each bowl: rice, chile chicken, black beans, a drizzle of cilantro-lime dressing, and any toppings you like.

    3 min

One Instant Pot: braise the chicken first, then cook the brown rice in the same pot using the reserved braising liquid, so the rice and chicken are sequential, not parallel. The chicken glaze, dressing and beans happen on the stovetop while the rice cooks. Brown rice is cooked at a 1:1.25 broth-to-rice ratio, 22 min high pressure plus a 10-minute natural release.
